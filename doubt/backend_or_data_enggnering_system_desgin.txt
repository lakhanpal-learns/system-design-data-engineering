Phase 4 — End-to-end projects

Design systems like:

Spotify Analytics Platform
Amazon Order Analytics
Digital Payment Analytics
Credit Risk Pipeline
Supply Chain Intelligence Platform (your current project)
Real-time Fraud Detection

Be able to explain:

Where the data comes from
How it's ingested
Where it's stored
How it's transformed
How it's orchestrated
How it's monitored
How dashboards consume it
How failures are handled

Based on what you've told me previously, your Supply Chain Intelligence Platform is already a good foundation for learning Data Engineering system design. Instead of only implementing it, spend time drawing and explaining its architecture—why each component exists, how data flows through it, what happens if a service fails, and how it could scale. That's the type of reasoning interviewers are often looking for in entry-level Data Engineering interviews.
